## Track 1 — honest candidate counts. DONE, awaiting merge.

Pull request: https://github.com/TommyMccormick12/voter/pull/28
Branch: `fix/2026-08-08-honest-candidate-counts`

The race-picker card and the scorecard header both rendered the count of
candidates we profiled as though it were the number of people on the ballot.
That is an affirmative false claim, not an omission.

Measured against live production before the fix:

| Race | Page said | On the ballot |
|---|---|---|
| FL-16 D | "3 candidates" | 5 |
| FL-16 R | "2 candidates" | 3 |
| FL-SEN R | "3 candidates" | 4 |
| FL-14 R | "1 candidate" | 8 |

Scale: 34 of the 43 contested races show fewer than 3 candidates, and 15 show
exactly one. With no disclosure, a contested primary reads as a coronation.

The change:

1. Both counts now read "N with policy data", which is what they measure.
2. Contested scorecards carry a coverage disclosure.
3. A `no_primary` race does NOT get the disclosure. There one candidate is the
   whole truth, and the line would contradict the badge from #27.

Gates: 496 tests pass (up 4), typecheck clean, build passes, lint 0 errors.
Verified on the preview deployment: the header reads "Aug 18, 2026 · 1 with
policy data", the bare count is gone, and the disclosure appears only on
contested races.

**This does not improve coverage.** It states the coverage we have honestly.
